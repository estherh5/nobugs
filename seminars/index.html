Our Recertification Seminars

NoBugs! completed its 36th year of hosting annual pesticide recertification webinars approved by the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture and the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection.

We will continue hosting webinars for our programs in 2027. We will update the dates, outlines and credits here as we have them. Enjoy the upcoming holidays and have a great new year!

You can call or email us with any questions or concerns about your credit needs.
